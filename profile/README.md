Founded by Jake Clarke (product and builds) and Gabriele Guarino (clients and delivery). Legal entity Symbaiotic Consultancy F.Z.E, UAE licence 50566, established January 2026. Clients include Mandala Creative Productions (Dubai), SHEfit and Mito Labs.
